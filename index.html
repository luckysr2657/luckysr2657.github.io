Welcome to Khumanpur Village

Khumanpur is a beautiful and peaceful village known for its strong sense of unity, togetherness, and community spirit. The people of Khumanpur are friendly, supportive, and always come together to celebrate important occasions and festivals.

One of the most respected and important places in our village is the Kala Rathod Temple, which holds a special place in the hearts of the villagers and reflects our faith and traditions. Khumanpur is also blessed with beautiful natural surroundings. A large pond (talab) is located near the village, adding to its peaceful and scenic environment. Agriculture is an important part of village life, and our farmers work hard throughout the year, growing different crops according to the seasons.

Festivals are celebrated with great joy and enthusiasm in Khumanpur. Ganesh Chaturthi, Diwali, and Holi are celebrated especially with the participation of people from across the village. These celebrations strengthen our relationships and preserve our rich traditions and culture. Khumanpur is not just a village; it is a community built on unity, tradition, nature, and togetherness. We are proud of our village and its people, and we warmly welcome everyone to learn more about the beauty, culture, and spirit of Khumanpur.

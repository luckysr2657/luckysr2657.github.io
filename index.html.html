About Khumanpar

A close-knit village with a proud heritage and peaceful natural surroundings.

Khumanpar is a beautiful village in Dungarpur district. Around 120 people live here in approximately 25 homes. The village is home to a Rajput community and is surrounded by greenery, mountains and the calm waters of its talab.

Its peaceful atmosphere, natural beauty and beautiful temple make Khumanpar a special place for the people who call it home.
